# Correction 01 to `hydraulic_inputs_assessment.md` (the original stays as the historical stage-1 record; nothing there was overwritten)

Root's review found five statements in the stage-1 assessment that are wrong or too strong. Corrected here with the evidence:

1. **Provider organic-matter domain.** The accepted provider accepts organic matter in **[0, 8] percent by weight** (`soil_texture_properties.py`, flag 8 "OM outside [0, 8] %", `OM_MAX_PERCENT`). I called 0.1 % "the provider's floor"; there is no such floor: 0.1 % was only the illustrative low value I entered, and 0 is also valid.
2. **Ks was not "exactly 2.0e-5 in every accepted row".** The values were printed rounded to five decimals; the actual returned values are MOAB A (OM 0.1 %) **2.1515e-5 m/s**, MOAB Bw (OM 0.1 %) **1.6643e-5 m/s**, MOAB Bw (OM 0.517 %) **1.5905e-5 m/s** (`probes/provider_probe_result.json`). Observation (iii) of section 4 is withdrawn.
3. **Refusals are specific to the screened pit-derived sets, not a universal impossibility.** What was shown: the JORN A and Bw sets built from the pit texture, the pit density values and the two OM alternatives I tried were refused (density factor outside [0.9, 1.3], density outside [1000, 1800] kg/m³, or final air entry outside [0, 33) kPa), and MOAB A with OM 1.034 % was refused. Other OM values, measured retention anchors or an air-entry override were not screened. The site PTF is NOT used for the approved diagnostic and is not bypassed, clamped or refitted.
4. **JORN Bw density sample.** The Bw bulk-density sample (23–33 cm) lies **inside** the Bw horizon (18–46 cm). I wrote that it extended below the horizon top; that was wrong. It does extend 3 cm below the model's 30 cm base (a statement about the model profile, not the horizon). The density 0.9035 g/cm³ remains outside the provider's [1000, 1800] kg/m³ range.
5. **Hash identities.** The MOAB megapit value `9c4f867b…` that I called the manifest sha is the sha256 of the **listing** (`DP1.00096.001_2014-02_listing.json`). The retrieval **manifest.json** sha256 is `cf78a39c1e668f24a8cf0341849c93ea8c42803d53a325962432d9b564f9f4fe`. The air manifests are pinned in the configs by their `manifest.json` sha (JORN `1db84000…`, see `config_proposals/*.json`).
6. **Residual and the column.** The historical fixed-layer column permits water below the residual content (no initial-state floor); the evaporation multiplier is zero at or below the residual. My wording "floor" in section 4 referred to OM, not to this; the corrected statement is: with a residual near the wilting point an initial shallow state at or below it would evaporate nothing, and nothing in the column forbids that state.
7. **Superseded decision.** The stage-1 proposals of section 7 (properties, residual, stress, K) are superseded by the user's approval of the generic EcoHyD reference soil (residual 0.01, evaporation stress 0.18, FC = sat = 0.26, Ks 6.9 mm/h, interface K from the EcoHyD constructor, user's three 10 cm layers). The reference soil has θ_sat = 0.26 well above every observed initial layer value (0.03–0.07), so the new initial-saturation check passes and no drainage is expected; this is a generic mechanism diagnostic, not site hydraulic validation. Everything else in the original assessment (pit tables, JORN A density conflict, `estimatedOC` ≠ OM, window limits) stands.
